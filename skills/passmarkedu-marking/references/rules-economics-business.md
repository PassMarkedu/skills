# Economics and business (CAIE 9708, Edexcel IAL WEC/WBS)

## Short-answer and data questions

- **K** (knowledge), **APP** (application to the context), **AN** (analysis: a chain of reasoning), **EV** (evaluation: a judgement weighed against something) are separate points. Award each only for what the candidate actually does — a definition is K, not AN; repeating the case data is not APP unless it is used.
- Calculations: `numeric` steps as in the sciences; copy the candidate's value, including units such as %, $m.
- Diagrams: correctly labelled axes, curves, equilibrium, and the shift the question asks about; these steps are `visual`.
