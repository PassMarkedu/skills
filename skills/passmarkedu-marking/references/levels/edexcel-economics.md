# Levels: Edexcel IAL Economics (WEC11–WEC14)

Levels-based items: data-response parts (e.g. 12(e), 14 marks = KAA 8 + EV 6) and essays (20 marks = KAA 12 + EV 8). Sources: Examiner Reports WEC11–14 June 2025.

- **KAA and EV are levelled separately, point by point.** Examiners level each paragraph ("L3", "L2+", "E2-") and add the **best two** KAA points and the best two EV points ("NB: take best two points", WEC12 Jun25 p.31; worked totals throughout WEC12/WEC14). If the unit has separate KAA and EV `level` steps, level each this way; with one step, use the combined total.
- **Depth over breadth.** Listing points is Level 1; a two-stage chain is Level 2; multi-stage chains reach Level 3 (WEC12 p.34). "Covering a lot of points in a superficial way will limit the mark to a low Level 2 at best" (WEC12 p.49; WEC14 p.52).
- Count valid causal links when assessing depth. A reversed or contradictory mechanism is not developed analysis merely because it has several stages; credit the correct reasoning by best fit.
- **Context is a cap in essays.** Without application to a country/industry an essay cannot reach Level 4 KAA ("capping candidates at the top of Level 3", WEC13 p.35; WEC12 p.34). Naming the industry or restating the stem is not context (WEC13 pp.39–43). Copying the extract in a data question is Level 1 (WEC12 p.28; WEC14 p.17). This board **does** cap the level for missing context.
- Choose the level by best fit, then apply the context cap. The cap sets a ceiling; it does not establish that the answer reaches that level.
- **Diagrams:** where the question asks for one, a relevant diagram used in the text is needed for the top level (WEC13 p.24); drawn but not referred to earns less.
- **Evaluation:** listed evaluation points are Level 1 (WEC12 p.34); an evaluative point needs a chain of reasoning tied to the analysis for E2 (WEC12 p.49). An informed judgement is required for the top EV level in the 20-mark essays (WEC12 p.49; WEC14 p.52), not in the 14-mark items. A summary conclusion earns nothing ("summary – no credit", WEC14 p.51).
- **"Benefits" questions:** positives are credited as KAA, problems and costs as EV (WEC11 p.35).
- Within a level, use the examiners' +/−: start in the middle, "+" for full development and context, "−" for missing stages.
