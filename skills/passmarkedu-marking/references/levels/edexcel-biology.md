# Levels: Edexcel IAL Biology (WBI11–WBI16) — 6-mark levels-based answers

Biology's 6-mark items are **true levels (0–3)**, unlike physics and chemistry. The level comes from the *kind* of statement, the mark within it from the *number* (MS WBI11/WBI15 additional guidance). Sources: Examiner Reports June 2025 WBI11, WBI13, WBI15, WBI16.

| Level | Marks | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–2 | describes the data / isolated knowledge (1 aspect = 1, 2 aspects = 2) |
| 2 | 3–4 | explains (1 explanation = 3, 2 = 4) |
| 3 | 5–6 | explanations linked to the data or context of the question (the question's own guidance names the discriminating points for 6) |

- **Describing is not explaining:** description alone stays at Level 1 however long (WBI11 p.15; WBI15 p.33). "Explain" items are marked by the number of relevant explanations — examiners advise to "write a little about a lot and not a lot about a little" (WBI11 p.17). This is the opposite of economics/business, where depth beats breadth.
- Use the question's graphs as well as knowledge (WBI15 p.96); vague words ("damage", "affects") or a missing direction of change do not earn the content (WBI15 pp.37, 39). A comment on methodology or statistics often reaches the top (WBI15 p.39).
- Follow the question's own additional guidance for which explanations count at each mark; put the level, the explanations counted and the reason in `note`.
