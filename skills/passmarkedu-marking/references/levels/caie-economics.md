# Levels: CAIE AS & A Level Economics (9708)

Levels-based items: Paper 2 essay (a) 8 marks (AO1 3, AO2 3, AO3 2) and (b) 12 marks (AO1+AO2 8 in Levels 1–3, AO3 evaluation 4); Paper 2 data parts with evaluation marks; Paper 4 essays (AO1+AO2 in Levels 1–3, evaluation up to 6 in E1/E2) and Paper 4 Q1(d) with one mark for a clear conclusion. Where the question has its own Table A / Table B grids in the scheme, those grids decide. Sources: Examiner Reports 9708 s25, w25, m25, m26.

- **One-sided (b) answers:** no more than mid-Level 2 for analysis and **no** evaluation marks (9708_m25_er p.3; w25 p.17; s25 p.18).
- **Diagram caps:** when the question asks for a diagram, a response without a correct, labelled, relevant diagram cannot go beyond Level 2 (s25 p.32; m25 p.10).
- **Question scope caps:** covering fewer cases than the question requires (one market structure, one policy) or ignoring its qualifier ("a high rate", "target inflation") holds analysis at Level 2 (w25 p.34; s25 p.14; m26 p.12). Confused core concepts hold it at Level 1 (s25 p.15).
- **Evaluation** must compare the strengths of the competing arguments and reach a justified judgement that answers the question; a summary, a bare "it depends on …", or weaknesses of single arguments are not evaluation (s25 pp.10–20; w25 p.17). Undeveloped one-line comments are E1 at most (w25 p.33; m25 p.10). Evaluation is always required in essays, and the 4-mark "consider" parts need some too (w25 p.33; s25 p.17).
- **Data questions:** evaluation not tied to the data earns 0 (w25 p.22); policies not in the text earn nothing (m25 p.9; m26 p.11).
- Facts stated without explanation earn little; a diagram or formula alone is AO1 (m25 p.3).
