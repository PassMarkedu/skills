# Levels: CAIE AS & A Level Business (9609)

Levels-based items: Paper 1 5(b)/6(b) and Paper 2 1(d)/2(d), 12 marks (K 2, App 2, An 2, Eval 6); Paper 3 12-mark evaluate (analysis 2, evaluation 6); Paper 4 (AO1 3, AO2 2, AO3 up to 8, AO4 up to 7). Skills are marked separately: more analysis does not raise evaluation (9609_w25_er p.15). Sources: Examiner Reports 9609 s25, w25, m25, m26.

- **Gate:** Paper 4 — no AO1 means no AO2–AO4 marks (s25 p.34). An answer that does not answer the question (NAQ) scores 0 (s25 p.36).
- **Judgement:** no clear judgement means **0** evaluation marks (s25 p.12; w25 p.21); a reasoned "it depends on …" or why a judgement cannot yet be made counts (m25 p.12; s25 p.18). The judgement must answer the question's own words ("most useful", "should"), and either direction is accepted if it follows from the analysis (m25 p.5; w25 p.45).
- **Context caps evaluation at Level 2:** Level 3 evaluation (5–6 of 6) needs the judgement in the business context; without it the evaluation "capped at L2 EVAL" (w25 p.4; s25 pp.1–3). This board **drops the level**, not just the mark. The sixth mark needs an overall judgement in context (s25 p.20). Repeating the business name or the question's words is not context; numbers from the case are (s25 p.14).
- **Balance** is needed for the higher evaluation marks (m25 p.10). "Analyse" items (P1 a, P2 c, P3 Q1–2) take no counter-argument or conclusion — such material earns nothing (s25 p.27; w25 p.32).
- One developed chain beats several list points (s25 p.12). Paper 4 Q1 evaluates what the business did (past tense); Q2 advises (m26 p.12).
