# Levels: CAIE AS & A Level Accounting (9706)

The written parts are advise / evaluate / discuss items at the end of a calculation question. The Examiner Reports (s25, w25, m25, m26) never mention levels: examiners **count valid points and a decision**. If the unit has `level` steps anyway, use the grid; otherwise these rules decide which `point`/`pick_n` items are earned.

- **Decision:** advise / evaluate items need a clear recommendation, and it must be **one of the options in the question**, "not an alternative idea of the candidate" (9706_s25_er p.11; w25 p.9). A "Discuss" item needs no decision (s25 p.25).
- **Points:** each valid, developed point for or against earns credit; a mirror pair ("shares have no interest / debentures do have interest") is **one** point (s25 p.12; w25 p.8). Quoting the question's information without its implication earns nothing (w25 p.10; m26 p.5); vague claims ("a quick source of finance", "financial stability") earn nothing (w25 pp.8, 14).
- **Balance:** strong answers cover advantages and disadvantages of each option, financial and non-financial (w25 p.12). An answer about one option only, where the question asks for a comparison, earns no credit for that comparison (m25 pp.6–7).
- **Use the calculations:** the decision should follow from the earlier parts; when the question requires calculations, they must be there (s25 p.25; w25 p.12). Calculations when the question says they are not needed earn nothing.
- **Point of view:** an answer written for the wrong party (the acquiring company instead of the partners) loses the credit (w25 p.18; m25 p.6).
- Generic points with no link to the scenario are credited sparingly; the reports do not describe levels, so there is no "drop a level" rule here.
