# Levels: Edexcel IAL Business (WBS11–WBS14)

Levels-based items: 8-mark discuss (3 levels), 10/12-mark assess (4 levels), 20-mark evaluate (4 levels). The whole answer gets one level. Sources: Examiner Reports WBS11–14 June 2025.

- **Method:** read the whole response, choose the best-matching level, start at its mid-point, move down for missing characteristics and up for a strong match (WBS12 p.10; worked 20-mark example: L4 mid-point 18, down to 17 because context was not sustained, p.31).
- **Balance:** discuss/assess/evaluate need a developed counter-argument, developed like the main points; "other factors should be considered" is not balance (WBS11 pp.11, 15).
- **Conclusion by command word:** 8-mark discuss — no conclusion required (WBS12 p.12); assess — the top level needs a supported judgement (WBS12 pp.12–15; WBS13 p.13); 20-mark evaluate — a judgement that adds something, not a summary (WBS13 pp.21, 26; WBS14 p.3).
- **Context usually moves the mark within the level:** "Some context but not supportive … throughout. Total marks = 8 (Low L4)" (WBS12 p.26); "bottom of Level 3" (WBS14 p.8). Little context **and** no balance drops to Level 2 (WBS11 p.15). Copying the extracts earns little or nothing (WBS11 p.21; WBS13 p.3).
- Quality over number of points; business concepts rather than "common sense" (WBS13 pp.13–32). Answering from the wrong perspective (economy instead of business) loses the credit (WBS14 p.3).
