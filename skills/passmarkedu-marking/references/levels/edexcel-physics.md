The starred 6-mark items are **not** holistic levels: indicative-content points (IC) plus structure marks, added (MS WPH11/WPH15, same table). Sources: Examiner Reports June 2025 WPH11, WPH13, WPH15, WPH16.

- **Count IC points strictly:** an IC counts only when all of its elements are there ("most said either hydrogen ran out or fusion ceased but not both", WPH15 p.72; "the Sun contracted but did not specify the core", p.72). Points may appear in any order.
- **Linkage:** 2 = coherent, logically structured, sustained reasoning throughout; 1 = partially structured with some linkage; 0 = unlinked points. Linkage cannot exceed the cap for the IC count — coherent writing never makes up for missing content (worked example: 3 IC + reasonable linkage = 2 + 1 = 3, WPH15 p.75).
- Relevant-sounding material that misses the question's point earns nothing (WPH15 p.72).
- If the scheme stores IC points as separate `point` steps plus one linkage step, judge each IC point and give the linkage step within the cap above; if it is one `level` step, send the total and list the IC points found in `note`.
