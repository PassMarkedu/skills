# Physics, chemistry, biology (CAIE 9702/9701/9700, Edexcel IAL WPH/WCH/WBI)

Science mark schemes are lists of **marking points**, not method/answer chains. In Edexcel chemistry and physics, "M1, M2, M3" are usually just the numbers of the points, not method marks: award each point that is present, independently, unless the scheme says one depends on another.

## Reading the scheme

- `/` separates acceptable alternatives; **ALLOW** / **accept** lists extra acceptable answers; **IGNORE** means that extra wording neither gains nor loses the mark; **REJECT** / **do not allow** / **not** means that wording loses the point even if the right idea is also there.
- **"any two from" / "max 2"**: these are `pick_n` steps — list which options the candidate states in `matched`; the server applies the maximum.
- A contradiction (a right and a wrong statement about the same point) scores 0 for that point.
- **Key words** in bold or underlined in the scheme must be present (e.g. "*electrons* are delocalised", "*per unit* charge").

## Calculations

- A wrong answer with no working earns nothing.

- These are usually `numeric` steps: copy the candidate's final value exactly (with the number of figures they wrote) into `value`; PassMarkedu checks it.
- **ecf / TE / "error carried forward" / "transferred error"**: a later step can be earned using the candidate's own wrong earlier value; judge the method with their numbers.
- **Units**: award only if the scheme asks for a unit and the candidate's unit is right; a missing unit loses only the mark the scheme ties to the unit.
- **Significant figures**: follow the scheme (often "2 or 3 s.f."); do not penalise extra figures unless the scheme says so.

## Practical and diagram questions

- Graphs: check axes labelled with quantity and unit, sensible scales (points occupy at least half the grid), points plotted to within half a small square, line/curve of best fit. These steps are `visual`.
- Apparatus diagrams: the scheme lists the features required; each is a point.

## Examiner conventions (Examiner Reports 2025–2026; quotes and pages in the bench's `records/examiner-reports/`)

- **Edexcel 6-mark extended answers:** read `references/levels/edexcel-<physics|chemistry|biology>.md` — physics and chemistry add indicative points and linkage marks; biology uses levels 0–3.
- **One answer asked, two given:** a wrong answer cancels a right one ("right + wrong = wrong"; "burette or measuring cylinder" earns nothing). In a numbered space only the first answer counts (Edexcel biology); where a list of N is asked, extra wrong items cancel correct ones (CAIE list rule). Contradictions score 0 for the point.
- **Physics:** a missing or wrong unit usually loses the final mark; a "show that" answer should carry one more significant figure than the value given; definitions must be dimensionally correct ("per unit", "product of"), not "stress by strain" (CAIE 9702). Deduce/evaluate items need calculation, comparison and conclusion for the last mark.
- **Chemistry:** carried-forward errors (TE/ecf) are credited only when the result is chemically sensible (no negative bond enthalpy); a missing sign on ΔH loses its mark; significant figures follow the least precise data (at least 2), and premature rounding loses marks; equations balance atoms **and** charge, ionic equations omit spectator ions, state symbols when required (CAIE also by default for ionisation energy and thermochemical equations); mechanism arrows start from a lone pair, bond or negative charge and end precisely; a formula written wrongly (KmNO4) earns nothing.
- **Biology:** exact terms and spellings that change the term are rejected (fibrogen, ATP synthase for ATPase, "control" for "control variable"); follow the required format (standard form, decimal places, whole numbers, direction of change); "no significant difference", never "insignificant"; repeating the stem or the pre-release text earns nothing.
- **CAIE practical papers:** plotted points larger than half a small square, or awkward scales, lose the plotting/axis marks; a gradient triangle uses points on the line more than half its length apart; the uncertainty in a gradient or intercept uses the worst acceptable line. Chemistry titration readings to .00/.05 cm³, concordant within 0.10 cm³, mean of titres within 0.20 cm³ given to 2 d.p. Biology tables carry units in headings only; drawings use clear continuous lines, no shading, most of the space, correct proportions.
