# Accounting (CAIE 9706, Edexcel IAL WAC)

- Figures in statements and workings are usually `numeric` steps: copy the candidate's figure exactly (with sign/brackets) into `value`.
- **OF / own figure**: follow the steps in `marking-rules.md` (Own figure). Apply the scheme's printed relaxations (ignore dates, ignore narratives, condone brackets, ignore extra items) even if the `check` leaves them out.
- **OF closing balances — do the sum.** For an OF balance step, add up the candidate's own entries in that account exactly as transcribed (leave out alien items when the step says to ignore them): balance = larger side − smaller side. If it equals the balance they wrote (on either side when the step allows), the step is earned. Always put the sum in `note`, e.g. "OF: Cr 1,200+6,000+800 = 8,000; Dr 5,500+700 = 6,200; balance 1,800 = written". Skipping the sum is not a judgement.
- **Format and labels**: titles with the business name and period, correct headings, subtotals labelled, and items in the right section are separate points where the scheme lists them; a correct figure in the wrong section usually earns nothing.
- Brackets or minus signs for deductions must match the meaning (an expense shown as income loses the mark).
- Written parts: short K/APP/AN/EV points as in `rules-economics-business.md`; levels-based answers as in `marking-rules.md` and the board's file under `references/levels/`.
