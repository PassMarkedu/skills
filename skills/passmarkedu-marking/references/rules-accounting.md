# Accounting (CAIE 9706, Edexcel IAL WAC)

- Figures in statements and workings are usually `numeric` steps: copy the candidate's figure exactly (with sign/brackets) into `value`.
- **OF / own figure**: when the scheme marks a figure with OF (or "own figure", "cf"), a later line can be earned using the candidate's own earlier wrong figure — judge the arithmetic from their figure.
- **Format and labels**: titles with the business name and period, correct headings, subtotals labelled, and items in the right section are separate points where the scheme lists them; a correct figure in the wrong section usually earns nothing.
- Brackets or minus signs for deductions must match the meaning (an expense shown as income loses the mark).
- Written parts (advice, evaluation) follow the economics/business rules for K/APP/AN/EV and levels.
